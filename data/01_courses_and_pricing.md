# Global Language Academy - Courses, Pricing, and Enrollment Dynamics Catalog

## 1. Course Offerings Overview
Global Language Academy offers premier language training designed according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Our programs are tailored for individual students, working professionals, teens, and corporate teams seeking conversational fluency and official international certification.

### Available Language Programs:
- **General English**: Levels A1 to C1. Focuses on speaking confidence, listening comprehension, grammar mastery, and real-world conversation.
- **Business & Professional English**: Levels B1 to C2. Geared towards executive communication, international negotiations, email writing, cross-cultural diplomacy, and pitch presentations.
- **French (Français)**: Levels A1 to C1. Follows the official CIEP / France Éducation International framework.
- **German (Deutsch)**: Levels A1 to B2. Aligned with the Goethe-Institut standards for academic migration, Studienkolleg, and work visas.
- **Italian (Italiano)**: Levels A1 to B2. Dynamic cultural immersion and language acquisition.
- **Portuguese (Português Brasileiro)**: Levels A1 to B2. Business and everyday conversational fluency.
- **Spanish for Foreigners (Español)**: Levels A1 to C2. Intensive immersion for expatriates, tourists, and digital nomads.

## 2. Group Courses vs. Private 1-on-1 Mentorship (How It Works)

### A. Group Courses Dynamics and Policy
- **Do I need to form my own group of 8 people?** 
  - **NO.** Individual students register individually. Global Language Academy forms and manages the cohorts based on each student's exact CEFR level (determined by the free online placement test).
  - Groups have a minimum of 4 and a strict maximum of **8 students per classroom** to guarantee high participation, individual feedback, and active speaking time.
  - If a company or private group of friends wants a closed corporate group, custom closed cohorts can be arranged upon request.
- **Are the tuition fees per person or per group?**
  - **All listed tuition fees are INDIVIDUAL PER PERSON per module/level.**
  - Fees include all official digital materials, license to the interactive platform, conversation club access, placement assessment, and certification diploma.
- **What happens if I miss a group class?**
  - All Live Online sessions are recorded in HD and uploaded to the student portal within 2 hours.
  - Students in on-campus or online group tracks receive **up to 2 free 1-on-1 tutoring recovery sessions (45 minutes each)** per 10-week module with an academic mentor to catch up on missed topics.
