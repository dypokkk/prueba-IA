# Global Language Academy - Courses and Pricing Catalog

## 1. Course Offerings Overview
Global Language Academy offers premier language training designed according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Our programs are tailored for students, working professionals, and corporate teams seeking fluency and official certification.

### Available Language Programs:
- **General English**: Levels A1 to C1. Focuses on speaking confidence, listening comprehension, grammar mastery, and real-world conversation.
- **Business & Professional English**: Levels B1 to C2. Geared towards executive communication, international negotiations, email writing, and pitch presentations.
- **French (Français)**: Levels A1 to C1. Follows the official CIEP / France Éducation International framework.
- **German (Deutsch)**: Levels A1 to B2. Aligned with the Goethe-Institut standards for academic migration and work visas.
- **Italian (Italiano)**: Levels A1 to B2. Dynamic cultural immersion and language acquisition.
- **Portuguese (Português Brasileiro)**: Levels A1 to B2. Business and everyday conversational fluency.
- **Spanish for Foreigners (Español)**: Levels A1 to C2. Intensive immersion for expatriates, tourists, and digital nomads.
