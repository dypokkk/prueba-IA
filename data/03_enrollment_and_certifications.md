# Global Language Academy - Enrollment, Levels, and Official Certifications

## 1. CEFR Level Framework and Progression

Our curriculum adheres strictly to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Each full level consists of two 60-hour modules (A and B):

| CEFR Level | Title & Competence | Modules Included | Total Hours | Expected Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | Breakthrough / Beginner | A1.1 & A1.2 | 120 Hours | Basic greetings, personal details, immediate needs |
| **A2** | Waystage / Elementary | A2.1 & A2.2 | 120 Hours | Everyday situations, routine descriptions, shopping |
| **B1** | Threshold / Intermediate | B1.1 & B1.2 | 120 Hours | Work & travel conversations, opinions, plans |
| **B2** | Vantage / Upper-Intermediate | B2.1 & B2.2 | 120 Hours | Complex professional discussions, technical fluency |
| **C1** | Effective Operational Proficiency | C1.1 & C1.2 | 120 Hours | Fluent academic debates, executive negotiation, idioms |
| **C2** | Mastery / Native-Level Precision | C2.1 & C2.2 | 120 Hours | Total mastery, nuanced literary and professional command |

## 3. Official Exam Preparation and International Certifications

Global Language Academy is an authorized preparatory center for world-renowned international examinations:

### Supported Certification Programs:
1. **IELTS (International English Language Testing System)**:
   - Formats: Academic (university admissions) and General Training (immigration to Canada, UK, Australia).
   - Preparation Course: 40 hours of intensive strategy + 4 full computer-delivered mock tests with band score analysis.
2. **TOEFL iBT (Test of English as a Foreign Language)**:
   - Preparation Course: 40 hours focused on integrated speaking and academic writing.
3. **Cambridge English Qualifications**:
   - B2 First (FCE) and C1 Advanced (CAE).
4. **DELF / DALF (French Ministry of Education)**:
   - Official certification diplomas for French (DELF A1-B2, DALF C1).
5. **DELE / SIELE (Instituto Cervantes)**:
   - Spanish language proficiency diplomas for foreign nationals.

*Upon finishing any regular level with an average score of 80/100 or higher, Global Language Academy issues an official digital diploma with verifiable QR code and blockchain verification.*
