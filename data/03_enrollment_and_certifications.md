# Global Language Academy - Comprehensive Enrollment, Certifications, and Academic Policies

## 1. Official CEFR Certifications and International Validity

- **Accreditation**: Global Language Academy is certified under Colombian Secretarías de Educación guidelines (SIET registry) and fully compliant with the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR).
- **Certificate Features**:
  - Direct digital verification via unique **QR Code** and permanent cryptographic record.
  - One-click **LinkedIn Digital Credential** integration.
  - Detailed transcript specifying oral fluency, reading comprehension, listening accuracy, and written grammar scores.
- **Homologation and University Validation**: Valid for graduation requirements in major Colombian, Mexican, and Latin American universities (UniAndes, Javeriana, UPB, EAFIT, UNAM, TEC).
- **Embassy & Visa Support Letters**: Official enrollment certificates and progress reports provided for student and work visas (Germany Opportunity Card, France Campus France, Canada Express Entry, Australia VET).

---

## 2. Free Diagnostic Placement Test

- **Format**: 45-minute comprehensive adaptive evaluation (grammar, vocabulary, listening comprehension, and a 10-minute conversational interview).
- **Score Mapping (0–100%) to CEFR Level**:
  - `< 30%` ➔ **A1.1 (Foundations / Beginner)**
  - `30% – 49%` ➔ **A2.1 (Elementary Fluency)**
  - `50% – 69%` ➔ **B1.1 (Intermediate Practical)**
  - `70% – 84%` ➔ **B2.1 (Professional Upper-Intermediate / IELTS Prep Ready)**
  - `85% – 94%` ➔ **C1.1 (Advanced Executive)**
  - `95% – 100%` ➔ **C2 (Mastery & Certification)**
- **Cost**: 100% Free with immediate digital report.

---

## 3. Course Freezing (Congelamiento) and Pause Policies

- **Eligibility**: Students experiencing travel, temporary illness, or work overload can freeze their course.
- **Maximum Duration**: Up to **60 continuous calendar days** per academic level.
- **Advance Notice**: Notice must be submitted at least **5 business days** prior to the desired freeze start date.
- **Cost**: **$0.00 (Completely Free)** for up to 1 freeze per enrolled level. Subsequent freezes incur a minor administrative reactivation fee of $50,000 COP.

---

## 4. Cancellation and Refund Policies

- **Full 100% Refund**: If cancellation is requested in writing at least **5 business days prior** to the official course start date.
- **Partial 70% Refund**: If requested during the **first week (first 5 calendar days)** of the module (30% retained for administration and digital license costs).
- **After First Week**: Non-refundable, but remaining hours can be transferred to family members or credited toward a future course term valid for 12 months.
