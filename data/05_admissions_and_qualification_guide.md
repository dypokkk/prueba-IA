# Global Language Academy - Comprehensive Admissions, Qualification, and Corporate Guide

## 1. Step-by-Step Enrollment and Registration Process

1. **Step 1 - Diagnostic Assessment**: Complete the free 45-minute placement test online or at any campus front desk to establish your exact CEFR entry sub-level (A1.1 through C2).
2. **Step 2 - Schedule & Modality Selection**: Choose your preferred shift (Early Bird, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Saturday Intensive) and format (On-Campus or 100% Live Online).
3. **Step 3 - Tuition Payment & Discounts**: Pay via PSE, Credit Card (up to 3 installments 0% interest), or Addi financing. Apply Early Bird (15% OFF) or Annual Bundle (25% OFF) if eligible.
4. **Step 4 - Platform & Materials Onboarding**: Receive Canvas LMS login credentials, Zoom classroom link, and Cambridge/Oxford digital textbook licenses within 15 minutes.
5. **Step 5 - Welcome Orientation & Class Start**: Attend induction session and begin communicative immersion.

---

## 2. Minor Student Enrollment (Kids & Teens Track)

- **Age Requirements**: Students ages 8 to 15 are placed in the specialized Kids & Teens Saturday immersion cohorts (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM).
- **Parental Consent**: Legal guardian must provide student identity document (Tarjeta de Identidad / Registro Civil) and sign the academic terms agreement.
- **Parent-Teacher Conferences**: Scheduled at mid-term (Week 6) and final term (Week 12) with personalized progress rubrics.

---

## 3. Corporate Sponsorships and B2B Agreements

- **Corporate Invoicing (Facturación Electrónica DIAN)**: Tax-compliant electronic invoices generated within 24 hours for corporate entities sponsoring employee language education.
- **Corporate Discounts**: 12% to 20% discount tiers based on company headcount enrollment.
- **Customized Corporate Cohorts**: Dedicated private groups tailored to company industry vocabulary (Software Engineering, Fintech, Legal, Oil & Gas, Healthcare).

---

## 4. International Students and Visa Requirements

- **Official Visa Support Certificates**: For international students moving to Colombia or Colombian students applying for study/work visas abroad, Global Language Academy issues certified study certificates, weekly hourly intensity verification, and tuition receipts recognized by consulates and embassies.
