# Global Language Academy - Schedules, Modalities, and Campuses

## 1. Class Schedules and Timetables

To accommodate working professionals and university students, Global Language Academy offers morning, evening, and weekend time blocks.

### A. Weekday Schedules (Monday to Thursday)
- **Early Morning Track (Madrugadores)**:
  - 6:30 AM to 8:30 AM (Colombia Time, UTC-5).
  - 4 days per week (Monday through Thursday), 2 hours per session (8 hours/week).
- **Morning Standard Track**:
  - 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM (Monday through Thursday).
- **Afternoon Track**:
  - 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM (Monday through Thursday).
- **Evening Executive Track (Nocturno)**:
  - 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM (Monday through Thursday).
  - Designed specifically for working executives and university students.

### B. Weekend Intensive Track (Saturday Immersion)
- **Saturday Intensive Schedule**:
  - 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM (5 continuous hours with a 20-minute coffee & conversation break).
  - Complete 60 class hours in 12 consecutive Saturdays.

### C. Private 1-on-1 Sessions Scheduling
- Custom schedule availability from Monday to Saturday between 6:00 AM and 9:00 PM.
- Rescheduling policy: Students may reschedule individual sessions with at least 12 hours prior notice without forfeiture.

## 2. Learning Modalities

Students can choose between three flexible delivery models:

### 1. In-Person On-Campus (Presencial)
Classes are delivered in modern, climate-controlled classrooms equipped with smart screens, acoustic isolation, and high-speed Wi-Fi.
- Max group size: 8 students per classroom.
- Complimentary coffee, tea, and study lounge access.

### 2. 100% Live Online (Virtual en Vivo)
Real-time interactive classes hosted on Zoom Education with digital whiteboards and breakout rooms.
- Real-time teacher interaction (not pre-recorded video lectures).
- Complete session recordings uploaded to the student portal within 2 hours of class completion.
- Unlimited 24/7 access to our AI-powered pronunciation lab and grammar practice modules.

### 3. Hybrid Blended (Híbrido)
- 50% on-campus workshops (speaking labs, debates, and group presentations) + 50% virtual live seminars.
- Ideal for hybrid workers and students commuting across the city.

## 3. Physical Campus Locations and Facilities

Global Language Academy maintains two flagship physical campuses in Colombia:

### Bogotá Flagship Campus:
- **Address**: Calle 63 # 9-45, Chapinero Central, Bogotá, Colombia.
- **Access**: 2 blocks from Calle 63 TransMilenio Station.
- **Facilities**: 14 smart classrooms, multi-language library, rooftop conversation café, and student coworking lounge.
- **Opening Hours**: Monday to Friday 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM | Saturdays 7:30 AM - 2:30 PM.

### Medellín Flagship Campus:
- **Address**: Carrera 43A # 7-50, El Poblado (near Parque del Poblado), Medellín, Colombia.
- **Access**: 10-minute walk from Poblado Metro Station.
- **Facilities**: 10 interactive classrooms, outdoor garden terrace for conversation clubs, and testing center.
- **Opening Hours**: Monday to Friday 6:30 AM - 8:30 PM | Saturdays 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM.

## 4. Attendance and Makeup Class Policies

- **Minimum Attendance**: 80% attendance is required to qualify for the end-of-level evaluation exam.
- **Justified Absences**: Up to 3 justified absences per module (medical excuse or certified work duty).
- **Free Makeup Sessions**: Students with justified absences receive two complimentary 45-minute 1-on-1 makeup tutoring sessions per quarter.
